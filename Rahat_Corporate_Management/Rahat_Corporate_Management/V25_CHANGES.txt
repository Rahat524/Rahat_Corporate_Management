V25 Permanent Cashier Closing Update

- Removed EMP ID, designation, department and branch strip from the top header.
- Header/sidebar workspace starts directly below menu and toolbar.
- Day-wise Closing Records now show 1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th Collection, Closing Cash, Total Cash Collected, System Sale, Shortage, Excess, Audit Status, Note/Remarks, POS and Card Remarks.
- Records are grouped date-wise.
- Shortage and Excess amounts calculate automatically from Cash Difference.
- Cash and POS remarks auto-generate when blank, but can be manually edited.
- Excel and PDF exports contain the same detailed columns.
- Manual closing accepts any quantity from 1 to 50; empty rows are ignored.

Use this V25 folder as the permanent working base for future updates.
